# Entity ImageGen Prompts

Use the visual reference:

- `assets/concept/zombie-liberation-art-bible-v2.png` — style/art-bible reference only.

Shared negative prompt for every entity:

> No readable text, no logos, no watermark, no blood, no gore, no wounds, no horror mood, no realistic weapons, no copyrighted characters, no exact national flags, no official emblems, no detached effects, no shadows, no smoke clouds, no speed lines, no confetti, no guide marks.

Shared style:

> Kid-friendly colorful 2D mobile-game sprite, compact full-body silhouette, bright clean palette, readable at small size, soft rounded forms, original character design, flat solid chroma-key background for background removal.

## Fighters

### Артем Блискавка

Lightning hero. Blue and yellow outfit inspired by energetic lightning shapes, gloves, confident friendly face, compact athletic silhouette. Lightning motif must be attached to costume, not floating effects.

### Софія Щит

Shield defender. Blue armor-like outfit with a toy-like crystal shield, protective pose, kind determined expression, sturdy compact silhouette. Shield is rounded and friendly, not sharp.

### Макс Ракета

Rocket-energy attacker. Orange/black adventure outfit with a toy-like rocket backpack or foam rocket gauntlet cue. Avoid firearm-like launcher. Excited brave expression, compact silhouette.

### Ліна Іскра

Spark tactician. Purple/pink outfit, tiny wand-like planning baton or star patch attached to costume, clever cheerful expression, agile compact silhouette. No floating stars outside body.

### Данило Лев

Lion finisher. Green outfit with lion mane hood or plush lion glove, bold happy grin, strong compact silhouette. Lion cue should be costume/plush, not a separate animal.

## NPCs

### Веселий зомбі

Silly non-scary zombie minion, greenish playful palette, awkward pose, big goofy eyes, rounded child-safe shape. Looks confused/funny, not dangerous.

### Зомбі-Бос Буль-Буль

Large silly zombie boss, round body, goofy crown-like toy hat or bubble-crown shape, exaggerated funny expression, purple/green palette, playful not frightening. Big silhouette for boss readability.

## State prompt suffixes

### idle

Four-frame idle row: calm breathing bounce, same full-body pose family, tiny readable movement, no detached effects.

### attack

Six-frame attack row: safe action wind-up and follow-through, no impact burst, no projectiles leaving body, no motion trails. Action should be readable through body pose only.

### hurt

Four-frame hurt row: surprised cartoon reaction, safe and funny, no wounds, no tears detached from face, no pain severity.

### victory

Four-frame victory row: cheerful celebration pose, no confetti, no floating symbols, no text.
